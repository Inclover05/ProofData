# ProofData Design References

## Reference 1 — Atmospheric Analytical Hero (proofdata-ref-1.png)

### WHAT WE BORROW
- Oversized editorial typography that makes a strong, confident statement.
- Large cinematic visual field for the hero section to establish the mood.
- Technical grid lines that subdivide the space and suggest precision.
- Floating analytical surfaces that provide depth and structure to data.
- Foreground/background depth using strategic layering.
- Controlled translucency for analytical overlays.
- Premium visual drama that elevates the product above standard SaaS.

### WHAT WE DO NOT COPY
- The specific branding or logo.
- The exact layout or composition of the page.
- The exact color palette (warm orange/yellow gradient).
- The exact hero art (the human silhouette).
- The exact card arrangement.
- Unrestrained glassmorphism across all elements.

### WHERE IT APPLIES IN PROOFDATA
- **Homepage (`/`) Hero Section**: Using the atmospheric field, technical grids, and controlled glass overlays to explain ProofData's core value proposition instantly and dramatically.

## Reference 2 — Institutional Financial Research (proofdata-ref-2.png)

### WHAT WE BORROW
- The undeniable seriousness of institutional research.
- Bold editorial question/headline framing to anchor the user's focus.
- Strong information hierarchy.
- Asymmetrical content structure that breaks the standard symmetric mold.
- Readable dense information suitable for complex analytical reports.
- Restrained grain/texture to add material reality.
- Black structural rules for clean separation.
- Financial-analysis credibility and authority.

### WHAT WE DO NOT COPY
- The exact branding or logo (FinSage).
- The pastel gradient backgrounds.
- The exact layout of the cards or prompt boxes.

### WHERE IT APPLIES IN PROOFDATA
- **Warrant Page (`/warrant/[id]`)**: Structuring the Reliance Warrant dossier to feel like a high-stakes financial or forensic report.
- **Create Page (`/create`)**: Presenting the risk/consequence and intended action forms with clear, serious hierarchy.

## Reference 3 — Swiss / Editorial Report System (proofdata-ref-3.png)

### WHAT WE BORROW
- Strict modular grid that organizes complex information predictably.
- Deliberate asymmetry for visual interest and dynamic tension.
- Generous whitespace that allows technical data to breathe.
- Metadata rails to hold secondary information without cluttering the main read.
- Thin rules to define structure elegantly.
- Document/report structure that feels like print material.
- Numbered information for clear sequencing.
- Strong typography with high contrast.
- Controlled accent color used specifically for semantic meaning (like verdicts).

### WHAT WE DO NOT COPY
- Direct reproduction of the layout or visual forms.
- The exact monochrome + vivid blue palette.
- The Pinterest/browser wrapper context.

### WHERE IT APPLIES IN PROOFDATA
- **The Reliance Warrant Dossier (`/warrant/[id]`)**: Providing the structural skeleton for how evidence, metadata, rules, and the final verdict are laid out.
- **Compare Page (`/compare`)**: Using the grid and thin rules to sharply delineate the exact same evidence resulting in two different outcomes based on consequence, making the distinction visually undeniable.
